# SignSight Learn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers the SignSight Learn app for Meta Quest, made and managed by the SignSight Learn team. In short:
**the app does not collect your personal data, has no account, and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.** Your
learning progress is kept only on your headset, and you can delete it at any time.

## 1. What data the app processes and stores

**Hand tracking (processed, not stored).** To teach and check signs, the app reads the positions of your hand joints
from Meta Quest hand tracking while you use it. From them it works out how bent each finger is, which way your palm
faces, where your hand is and how it moves. This happens on the headset, in real time, while you sign. The hand data
is not saved to a file and is not sent anywhere.

**Learning progress (stored on your headset only).** The app keeps one small file on the headset
(`learner_profile.json`) so the Tutor can choose what you practise next. For each sign it records numbers such as how
many times you tried it, how many tries were correct, which finger was most often wrong, the movement hint you were
given, and when you last saw it. It contains no name, email, account, photo, video, audio or location.

**What the app does not use.** The app has no camera, microphone or face tracking access. It does not read room or
scene data. It has no user account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

## 2. How the app uses this data

- **Hand tracking data** is used only to recognise the sign you make and to show feedback, such as which finger to
  bend or how to move.
- **Learning progress** is used only by the in-app Tutor to decide which sign comes next and which finger to
  highlight, and to keep your progress between sessions.

The data is never sold, shared, sent to a server or used for any other purpose. The app makes no network requests
of its own.

## 3. How to delete your data

All data the app stores is on your own headset, so you can delete it yourself at any time, free of charge:

- **Uninstall SignSight Learn** from your headset. This deletes the app together with `learner_profile.json`.
- Or clear the app's stored data in your headset's settings (Settings > Apps > SignSight Learn > Storage), if your
  headset version offers this.

We do not hold any copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no request is needed. If
you still have a question or a request, open an issue at https://github.com/alexwangdev/SignSight/issues and we will
answer it. Deletion is never charged for.

## 4. Meta platform

Hand tracking itself is provided by your Meta Quest headset and its operating system. How Meta handles data on its
platform is covered by Meta's own privacy policy (https://www.meta.com/legal/quest/privacy-policy/), not by this one.

## 5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.
